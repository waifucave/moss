> **Not released yet.** This page describes MOSS as it is being built for the first release.

Open a bot's side panel with the button at the top right of its conversation. At its top are the bot's settings and a button to close it.

## Its screen

When the bot uses a tool that shows its work, such as its browser, the panel shows that work live, marked **Live**, with the name of the tool showing it. It is **watch only**: clicking or scrolling on it does nothing, so you can't press anything in the bot's place by accident. Taking over a bot's screen comes in a later release.

With nothing to show, the panel says so: *Nothing on* the bot's *screen*.

The bot's browser works in the background, apart from yours: it doesn't open windows on your screen, and it doesn't share your browser's sign-ins.

## Its routines

Below the screen, **Routines** lists the bot's routines, each with its schedule and whether it's on, with **Run now**, **Pause** or **Resume**, and **Edit**. [Routines](/docs/routines/) explains them.
