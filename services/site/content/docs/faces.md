> **Not released yet.** This page describes MOSS as it is being built for the first release.

## A name makes a face

Every bot is one flat shape in one colour, with two eyes. Its name sets four traits, and the same name always makes the same face, on every computer:

| Trait | Choices |
| :- | :- |
| **Shape** | Ball, Drop, Dome, Bowl, Pill, Loaf, Tile, Peak, Gem, Cup |
| **Colour** | one of ten |
| **Eyes** | Round, Tall, Dots, Calm |
| **Temper** | Steady, Restless, Slow, Bright: how lively the face is |

MOSS, the bot you start with, is the coral tile with round eyes, the same face as the app's icon.

The faces are alive: they blink and look about. With your system's *reduce motion* setting on, they move less, with no jumps or turns.

## Pinning a face

To change how a bot looks, open its settings and find **Face**. Pick a shape, a colour, eyes or a temper, and that trait stays as you chose it, whatever the name makes. **From the name** puts a trait back.

## Moods

A face shows what its bot is doing, and the word for it is always beside it:

| Mood | When |
| :- | :- |
| **Idle** | waiting for you |
| **Listening** | hearing you while you hold the talk button |
| **Thinking** | working out what to do |
| **Working** | using a tool: files, commands, the web, its browser |
| **Needs you** | a card or a question is waiting for you |
| **Stuck** | it hit a problem it can't get past alone |
| **Done** | finished what it was doing |
| **Asleep** | paused, or out of reach |
