title: Pairing your devices

> **Not released yet.** This page describes MOSS as it is being built for the first release.

Pair two of your computers once, and a bot can work on either of them: its files and commands run on the computer that holds them, under that computer's own approval level. You don't need an account to pair.

## Pair two computers

On both computers, open MOSS's Settings (press **You** at the bottom of the list of bots) and find **Devices**, then **Pair a device**.

1. On the first computer, press **Show a code**. MOSS shows a code, a picture of it to scan with a phone's camera, and until when it works. A code works for up to 10 minutes.
2. On the second computer, type the code in **Code from another device** and press **Join**.
3. A card now asks you to compare **four words** with the other device's screen. The words are on the card, not on the settings page. When the four words are the same on both screens, press the card's button. If they differ, don't: someone else may be in the middle.

That's it: the two computers are paired. A code that ran out says *The code stopped working*; show a new one to try again. **Cancel** stops a pairing you started.

The same steps pair a phone, once the [phone apps](/docs/phones/) are out: the phone scans the picture, then you compare the four words.

## Your devices

**Devices** lists each paired device with its kind (*Computer*, *Phone*, *Tablet*, *Cloud computer*, *Browser*) and *online now* or when it was last seen. The computer you are on says *(this computer)*.

Your devices talk to each other over one encrypted link per pair, directly where they can and through a relay where they can't. [Relays](/docs/relays/) says how to choose one, and [What leaves your computer](/docs/privacy/#paired-devices) what each service involved can see.

## Removing a device

Press **Remove** beside it and answer the card. The computer ends that device's pairing and cuts its link at once; the device can't reach it again until it is paired anew. Do this for a lost phone or a computer you no longer use.
